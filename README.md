PartyPot – Smart Expense Sharing & Budget Management
Problem Statement

Managing shared expenses during trips, parties, college events, and group activities can be confusing and time-consuming. People often struggle to track who paid, how much each person owes, and how to settle the final balances. PartyPot solves this problem by automatically splitting expenses, calculating individual balances, tracking budgets, maintaining expense history, and providing simple settlement suggestions.
